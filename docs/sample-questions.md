# Sample Questions for the Chat UI

10 questions you can ask in the Streamlit chat, with the English and
Indonesian versions and the regulation(s) they target. Use these to try
the app quickly or to demo it to reviewers.

| # | English | Bahasa Indonesia | Regulation(s) covered |
|---|---------|------------------|-----------------------|
| 1 | What is QRIS and who can be a QRIS payment service provider? | Apa itu QRIS dan siapa saja yang dapat menjadi penyelenggara jasa pembayaran QRIS? | PADG 3/2025 (QRIS), PADG 32/2025 (PJP) |
| 2 | What are the key requirements for AI governance in Indonesian banks? | Apa saja persyaratan utama tata kelola AI di perbankan Indonesia? | OJK AI Governance for Indonesian Banking 2025 |
| 3 | What cybersecurity obligations do banks have under SEOJK 29/2022? | Apa saja kewajiban keamanan siber bank berdasarkan SEOJK 29/2022? | SEOJK 29/2022 (bank cyber security) |
| 4 | How must banks handle customer complaints under POJK 22/2023? | Bagaimana bank harus menangani pengaduan nasabah berdasarkan POJK 22/2023? | POJK 22/2023 (consumer protection) |
| 5 | What is the minimum IT risk management framework a bank needs? | Apa saja komponen minimum kerangka manajemen risiko TI yang wajib dimiliki bank? | POJK 11/2022 (bank IT), PADK 1/2026 (bank IT) |
| 6 | What anti-fraud strategies does POJK 12/2024 require? | Strategi anti-fraud apa yang diwajibkan oleh POJK 12/2024? | POJK 12/2024 (anti-fraud strategy) |
| 7 | What is BI-FAST and how does it affect payment services? | Apa itu BI-FAST dan bagaimana pengaruhnya terhadap layanan pembayaran? | PBI 10/2025 (payment systems industry) |
| 8 | What are the digital maturity levels in SEOJK 24/2023? | Apa saja tingkat kematangan digital dalam SEOJK 24/2023? | SEOJK 24/2023 (digital maturity) |
| 9 | What does the P2SK law (UU 4/2023) change for financial sector supervision? | Apa yang diubah UU 4/2023 (P2SK) terhadap pengawasan sektor keuangan? | UU 4/2023 (P2SK financial sector law) |
| 10 | What are the requirements for banks using fintech innovation (ITSK) under POJK 30/2025? | Apa saja persyaratan bank dalam menggunakan inovasi teknologi sektor keuangan (ITSK) berdasarkan POJK 30/2025? | POJK 30/2025 (ITSK fintech innovation) |

## Tips

- The chat supports both languages. Toggle English / Bahasa Indonesia
  in the sidebar to switch the UI language; answers and citations adapt.
- Typing in either language works - query rewriting expands acronyms
  (e.g. KPMM, ITSK, PJP) before retrieval.
- Tick "Use session memory (Nyawa)" to see related past questions
  recalled and to save questions for one-click re-asking.
- After each answer you can vote 👍 / 👎; this feeds the Grafana
  monitoring dashboard.
